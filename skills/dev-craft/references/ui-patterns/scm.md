# SCM (supply chain / inventory)

**Screens:** inventory by item and location; stock movements; purchase orders; receiving; shipping;
warehouses and locations; suppliers; replenishment.
**Core rule:** a stock-movement ledger; never overwrite quantities, derive balances from movements.
**Views:** on-hand/reserved/available per location, lot and serial tracking, low-stock and reorder alerts,
supplier performance (lead time, fill rate), demand vs stock chart.
**Receiving/shipping:** scan-friendly forms, partial receipts, discrepancy handling.
**Mistakes:** editing quantity directly; no location dimension; no way to trace a lot; alerts with no threshold setting.
