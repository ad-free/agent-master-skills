# HRM

**Screens:** dashboard; employee directory; profile; leave (requests, calendar, balances); attendance;
org chart; payroll; approvals.
**Dashboard widgets:** KPI cards (headcount, on leave today, open requests, turnover); headcount trend (line);
department breakdown (bar or donut, few slices); pending approvals list with approve/reject; quick actions.
**Directory:** table with filters (department, status, location), search, avatars, status badges, saved views.
**Profile:** header (photo, role, manager) with tabs: overview, job history, leave, documents, compensation
(permission-gated).
**Leave:** team calendar with colors per type; balance cards; request form that shows the remaining balance.
**Attendance:** heatmap by day; late/absent counts.
**Payroll:** period table, totals row, export; sensitive fields hidden without permission.
**Mistakes:** salary shown to everyone; free-text department; no empty/loading states; approvals buried in menus.
Data model hints: effective-dated compensation, audit trail (`data-modeling.md`).
