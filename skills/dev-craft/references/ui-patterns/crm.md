# CRM

**Screens:** pipeline board; contact and company records; deal detail; activity; forecast; settings.
**Pipeline:** kanban by stage with value totals per column, drag to move, card shows owner avatar and next step.
**Record page:** summary header, activity timeline (emails, calls, notes), related deals, tasks.
**Also:** lead scoring badge, email/call logging, deal forecast (bar by month vs target), saved views and
filters on every list, duplicate detection on create.
**Mistakes:** no timeline; moving a card without confirming required fields; forecast with no definition of
"weighted"; no bulk actions.
