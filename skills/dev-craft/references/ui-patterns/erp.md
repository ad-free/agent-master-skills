# ERP

**Screens:** master data (items, partners, chart of accounts); documents (quotes, orders, invoices, bills);
approvals; reports; period close.
**Documents:** status workflow draft -> approved -> posted with visible state and allowed transitions; posted
documents are immutable (reverse, do not edit); line-item grid with totals and tax; attachments; audit trail.
**Cross-cutting:** multi-company and multi-currency selectors, role-based approvals with delegation, bulk
import/export, period closing that blocks back-dated posting.
**Mistakes:** editing posted documents; money as float; no audit trail; approvals without a record of who and when.
